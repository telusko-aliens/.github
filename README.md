### 🛸 About Telusko

</div>

<p align="center">
Telusko is an education platform founded by <b>Navin Reddy</b>, helping millions of learners across the globe become job-ready developers. We believe in making technology simple, fun, and accessible to everyone.
</p>

<p align="center">
This is the official GitHub home for all our <b>course source code</b>, <b>project repositories</b>, and <b>learning resources</b>. Everything here is open for you to explore, practice, and build along with our tutorials.
</p>
